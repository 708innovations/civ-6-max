# Max - A New Civilization Mod

This document outlines the unique abilities, units, districts, and improvements for the Max civilization.

## Unique Leader Ability: Kleptomania

* Each city center starts with 5 product slots.

### Unique Unit Action: Pocket Silverware

* **Usable by:** Any unit in any foreign district.
* **Effect:** Grants one of the following products:
  * **Fork:** +2 Culture
  * **Spoon:** +2 Science
  * **Plate:** +1 Culture, +1 Science
* **"Loaded" City Bonus:** When a city has one of each (Fork, Spoon, Plate), it gains +1 Amenity.
* **Product Slot Expansion:**
  * Creation of a Seaport adds +3 product slots.
  * Creation of a Stock Exchange adds +3 product slots.
  * **Fully Loaded (2 Spoons, 2 Forks, 2 Plates):** Yields +2 Amenities.
  * **Overloaded (3 Spoons, 3 Forks, 3 Plates):** Yields +3 Amenities.

## Unique Civilization Ability: Soupmeister

* Unlocks the unique city center project: **Make Egg Drop Soup**.
  * **Completion Time:** 6 turns.
  * **Upon Completion:**
    * +1 Population in that city.
    * +1 Amenity for 6 turns in that city.
    * All your cities within a 6-tile radius get +10% Food for 6 turns.
* **Permanent Granary Bonus:** Every time "Make Egg Drop Soup" is completed, the city permanently gains +1 Food to its Granary, stacking up to +3.
* **Project Speed Boost:** When the Granary bonus reaches +3 Food, the "Make Egg Drop Soup" project only takes 4 turns to complete.

## Unique District or Building: Neighborhood - The Dylan

* **Effect on Construction:** Spawns 3 barbarian tanks around The Dylan when built.
* **Amenities:** -2 Amenities.
* **Housing:** +200 Housing.

## Unique Units

### Naked Homeless Man

* **Damage:** Does 5 damage every turn to nearby enemy units within 1 tile.
* **Ranged Attack:** Can throw forks, spoons, or plates as a ranged attack.
* **Movement:** Can cross closed borders.
* **Charges:** Has 2 charges per unit.
* **Grievances:** Each charge used adds 100 grievances against the city center it was activated in.
* **Persistence:** Unit does not die after using charges.

### Nuclear Satellite

* **Replaces:** Observation Balloon.
* **Class:** Support class air unit (can only be attacked by air combat or anti-air defense).
* **Unlock:** Unlocked by Astronomy.
* **Sight:** Has Sight of 9.
* **Sight Obscurity:** Sight is unobscured by terrain features.
* **Adjacent Unit Bonus:** Adds +1 Range to adjacent bombard class units.
* **Construction:** Can be built in an Aerodrome or Encampment district.

## Unique Improvement: Soup Kitchen

* **Production Bonus:** You get +30% production towards building a Naked Homeless Man.
* **Experience Bonus:** Naked Homeless Men get +50 experience on being built.
* **"Make Egg Drop Soup" Project Enhancement:** The "Make Egg Drop Soup" project now gives all cities in a 6-tile radius +20% Food for 6 turns (instead of +10% food).
